Inspired by the combination of stacked bowls with hip roofs to achieve a visually striking appearance, I employed computational modeling in Grasshopper and Rhino 8 to develop this project, named Cupcake. Twisting each wooden blade facade and introducing a 45% diagonal axis added a unique touch. I explored various versions of Cupcake in three backgrounds using Twinmotion’s [Path Tracer](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/en-us/twinmotion/path-tracer-requirements-for-twinmotion). With computer design, Cupcake features palm trees for illustration on top and comprises four bowl-shaped sides.

You can also check the design on my [TikTok](https://www.tiktok.com/@arnottferels/photo/7332762183811484933).
