Inspired by the dynamic model created in my ITB master’s [thesis](https://arnottferels.github.io/work/x-transit-hub/) on architecture, where I utilized the [SubD](https://www.rhino3d.com/features/subd/) workflow to model it in [Rhino 7](https://www.rhino3d.com/), I made adjustments to adapt it for creating a monochromatic version through the [Path Tracer](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/en-us/twinmotion/path-tracer-requirements-for-twinmotion) in [Twinmotion](https://www.twinmotion.com/en-US).
