Summary

Computational designer and design technologist with experience in architecture, computational methods, and digital systems. Skilled in developing models, workflows, algorithms, and simulations using generative and data-driven design approaches. Experienced in building extensible, user-configurable, user-informed, and context-aware computational frameworks. Focused on enabling designers to shape and customize their tools beyond basic use.
