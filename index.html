Arnott integrates design, computation, and technology to redefine architecture.

As a designer and researcher, he is deeply curious about cities, modular systems, and human behavior. His work focuses on creating spaces that are not only adaptable but also sustainable, responding to the evolving needs of both the environment and the people.
