Arnott Ferels

I am a computational designer and design technologist.

My work focuses on architecture, computational methods, and digital systems.

I work on models, workflows, algorithms, and simulations, using generative and data-driven approaches to design.

I build frameworks that are extensible, user-configurable, user-informed, and context-aware. I’m interested in how designers can help shape and customize the tools they use, not just operate them.
