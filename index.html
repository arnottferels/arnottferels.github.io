Hi!

I’m Arnott Ferels.

I’m a designer and researcher in computation, architecture, and technology, focused on the intersection of urban sustainability, human behavior, and modular systems. This intersection drives me to explore new solutions and ideas aimed at impacting communities, spaces, and built environments.
