Common Questions

About this site#

What’s this site for? #

This site is the hub for my works, posts, research, and collections. It also shows my portfolio.

How do I contact you? #

You can reach me anytime via my contact page.

How do I give feedback or report a bug? #

I’m open to suggestions. Leave comments on specific posts (like here or here). For general feedback, use my contact page.

How do I get updates? #

Check the homepage for new posts. You can subscribe to my RSS feed or follow me on social media for updates.

Do you track personal info? #

No, this site doesn’t collect personal data. See Privacy Policy for details.

Features and settings#

How do I move around the site? #

Use the top nav bar, footer, or Quick Links. To browse collections, go to Collections. Keyboard users can press / or press this Search button.

Can I switch the theme? #

The default theme is Auto. You can also switch to Dark or Light. To change it, open Settings, press Alt+T (Windows) / ⌥ T (Mac), or click this theme button.

Can I change the border style? #

The default style is Square. You can also switch to Round. To change it, open Settings, press Alt+E (Windows) / ⌥ E (Mac), or click this style button.

How do I switch card view on Collections? #

The default view is Grid. You can also switch to List or Comfort. To change it, open Settings, press Alt+V (Windows) / ⌥ V (Mac).

Note: All collections are on the Collections page.

How do I cite something from this site? #

On the page you want to cite, press Alt+C (Windows) / ⌥ C (Mac) to open the Cite popup. It shows formats like Harvard, APA, Vancouver, and BibTeX. You can copy or download them.

What tools are used to build and run this site? #

This site is built with Astro (5.12.9). It uses Pagefind (1.3.0) for . Comments, page views (v2), and basic interaction data are stored in Supabase. For page views (v2), it uses Supabase with Netlify Functions. For page views (v1), it uses GoatCounter.
