# Inspiration and Pattern Generation

## Pyramid (Option 1)

![Inspiration: Pyramid](https://arnottferels.github.io/a/img/714/Efrn.webp)

_Figure: Inspiration: Pyramid_

In Option 1 Pyramid Design, systematic iterations produced intricate 2D patterns extended into the third dimension (Z-coordinate). Weaving methods like (0-1-1-0) and (0-1-0-1) enhanced pattern manipulation across multiple dimensions.

![Pattern Generation: Pyramid](https://arnottferels.github.io/a/img/723/m5C3.webp)

_Figure: Pattern Generation: Pyramid_

![Pattern Generation: Pyramid (Perspective)](https://arnottferels.github.io/a/img/729/KJT7.webp)

_Figure: Pattern Generation: Pyramid (Perspective)_

## Wave (Option 2)

![Inspiration: Wave](https://arnottferels.github.io/a/img/714/Hjvp.webp)

_Figure: Inspiration: Wave_

In Option 2 Wave Design, a rattan object resembling a flag acts as an elegant ceiling ornament. The client’s request for visibility, especially from the bed, enhances the space with a dynamic and visually appealing element.

![Pattern Generation: Wave](https://arnottferels.github.io/a/img/717/CVfl.webp)

# Design and Details

## Pyramid

![Design Details: Pyramid](https://arnottferels.github.io/a/img/1521/iZVh.webp)

_Figure: Design Details: Pyramid_

Based on the iterations conducted, the axonometric design of Option 1 Pyramid is composed of several 4x6 panels, totaling 24 panels arranged diagonally.

## Wave

![Design Details: Wave](https://arnottferels.github.io/a/img/1509/APrN.webp)

_Figure: Design Details: Wave_

Based on the iterations conducted, the design of Option 2 Wave consists of 3 elongated panels locked along their length.
