# Introduction

An innovative housing project in [East Jakarta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/East_Jakarta) repurposes a vacant plot initially designated for high-rises. Strategically located near TransJakarta bus stops, mosques, and schools, the site leverages Jakarta’s container accessibility. Challenges include ergonomic adjustments and affordable housing demand. Opportunities involve surplus containers, Gen Y and Z entering the market, and addressing wealth gaps. The project aims to meet Jakarta’s housing needs by capitalizing on container advantages and embracing the city’s unique characteristics.

# Concept

## Self-economy

![Self-economy](https://arnottferels.github.io/a/img/1485/UTjN.webp)

_Figure: Self-economy_

The communal spaces on each floor deck and atrium, inspired by Indonesian culture, encourage a sense of togetherness and gathering. The Indonesian cultural characteristics of the market concept in the Kampong Container (Container Village) can also contribute to the local economy.

## Self-energy

![Self-energy](https://arnottferels.github.io/a/img/711/I5Kn.webp)

_Figure: Self-energy_

The rooftop and decks of each floor are equipped with ecological independent energy sources such as vertical gardens, solar panels, fish ponds, and wind turbines.
